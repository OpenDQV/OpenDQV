# Trademark Policy

The OpenDQV Core code is free to use under the MIT licence. This policy covers
something that licence does not: the OpenDQV name and logo. It exists so that
anyone who sees the name can tell who maintains the project.

## The code and the name are separate

The code in this repository is licensed under the [MIT licence](LICENSE). You may
use, change, distribute and host it on the terms of that licence.

The MIT licence covers the code only. It does not give you any right to use the
OpenDQV name or logo.

## Who owns the name and logo

OpenDQV™ is a trade mark of BGMS Consultants Limited (UK trade mark application
UK00004460333, classes 9 and 42). The OpenDQV logo is a brand asset of
BGMS Consultants Limited.

## What you may do without asking

- Refer to OpenDQV truthfully, for example "built on OpenDQV Core",
  "compatible with OpenDQV contracts" or "a fork of OpenDQV Core".
- Redistribute unmodified official releases under their original name.
- Write, teach, speak and make community content about OpenDQV, as long as you
  do not suggest that the project has made it or endorses it.

## What needs written permission

- Using "OpenDQV" in the name or branding of a product, a hosted or managed
  service, a company or a domain name.
- Offering a hosted service in a way that suggests it is OpenDQV, or that the
  project runs or endorses it.
- Using the OpenDQV logo in the branding of a product or service.
- Distributing a modified version under the OpenDQV name.

## Forks

You are welcome to fork OpenDQV Core. Please give your fork a different name,
and say that it is based on OpenDQV Core.

## The official managed service

by BGMS Consultants Limited, the maintainer of OpenDQV.
